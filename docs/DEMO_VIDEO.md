# SalesOps AI — 60–90 Second Demo

## Before Recording

Run `cd backend && python -m app.seed --reset --with-intelligence`, start the stack, and open the Dashboard at desktop width. Close unrelated browser tabs and notifications.

## Script

### 0–10 seconds — Dashboard

**Action:** Show the KPI row and qualification/pipeline charts.

**Narration:** “SalesOps AI turns incoming B2B leads into a clear, actionable sales workflow. The dashboard shows live qualification, pipeline value, won opportunities, and pending follow-up work.”

### 10–20 seconds — Lead Table

**Action:** Open Leads and search for `OrbitFlow`.

**Narration:** “Every lead is scored by the backend using transparent budget, company-size, and business-need rules, then classified as Hot, Warm, or Cold.”

### 20–35 seconds — OrbitFlow CRM Record

**Action:** Open Michael Brown at OrbitFlow SaaS and point to the 90/100 score, reasons, and Qualified stage.

**Narration:** “OrbitFlow is a Hot, Qualified opportunity. The record keeps its profile, explainable score, stage, tasks, and complete activity history together.”

### 35–50 seconds — Sales Intelligence

**Action:** Scroll through qualification summary, buying signals, risks, and next best action.

**Narration:** “The intelligence layer uses only known lead and workflow data to produce evidence-backed buying signals, risks to validate, and a reasoned next best action. It is clearly presented as an AI-assisted recommendation.”

### 50–60 seconds — Follow-up

**Action:** Click Copy on the suggested follow-up.

**Narration:** “It also prepares a personalized follow-up that a representative can review and copy into a future email or CRM integration.”

### 60–70 seconds — Task

**Action:** Create a high-priority follow-up task due tomorrow.

**Narration:** “The recommended action can immediately become trackable sales work with priority and due-date management.”

### 70–80 seconds — Pipeline

**Action:** Open Pipeline and move OrbitFlow from Qualified to Contacted.

**Narration:** “Pipeline movement uses the real backend workflow, not local mock state.”

### 80–90 seconds — Timeline and Dashboard

**Action:** Return to OrbitFlow, show the new stage-change activity, then finish on Dashboard.

**Narration:** “Every transition is recorded automatically, so the team keeps context while management retains a live operational view.”
